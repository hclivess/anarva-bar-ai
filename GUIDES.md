# Strategy guides: principles (not build orders)

What published guides say about *why* players decide as they do. Reasons only: every decision in Anarvā is computed
from live game facts, never copied from a guide (CLAUDE.md). A principle here is a hypothesis until the replays
(`runs/replays.db`) show it. Collected 2026-11-26.

No Total Annihilation design document is public; Chris Taylor's interviews describe intent, not mechanics.

## Opening and economy
- **Extractors first, then energy, then the factory, then radar/scouting**; metal is scarce, energy plentiful but
  crucial (BAR "To get started"). Replays agree: winners' commander builds 3 extractors, 2 solars, lab at ~61 s.
- **Expansion is the core strategy** because extractors are cheap and fast (BAR wiki, Expansion). Too little army and a
  raid takes half the base; too much and expansion slows; a fast, lightly defended expansion that accepts losing cheap
  outer extractors while the core is defended is preferred. In 1v1, expand sideways and watch the flanks.
- **Assist the lab** with the commander and constructors (BAR "To get started"). Replays: winners' commanders REPAIR
  33-39% of minutes 1-5.
- **Don't stall energy**: laser weapons need it (BAR tips for TA/SupCom players).
- **T2 is almost always more cost-efficient than T1**; reclaim T1 to fund fusion; convert overflowing energy into metal
  (BAR tips). TA guide: mid-game energy-to-metal conversion beats extractors that are hard to protect.
- **Reclaim** of wrecks often matters more than economy in competitive play (BAR tips).

## Factory and units
- **Bot lab**: cheap, climbs hills, good for early scouting and expansion, fragile. **Vehicle plant**: stronger,
  slower. **Air**: costly and weak, surprise, best once established (BAR "To get started"). Replays (28 Armada-vs-Cortex
  1v1 winners): Pawn/Rover/Flash early, Flash 42% of army metal at 4-8 min, Stumpy and air from 8 min.
- **Every unit has a counter**; keep builders or rez bots with the front squad to repair, reclaim and resurrect (BAR tips).
- **Rush** (TA guide): all-in on cheap fast attackers (the Flash) keeps the opponent reacting; the cost is slow expansion.

## Army and fights
- **Attack only when you're sure to get through**: a lost brawl donates metal to the enemy as wrecks (BAR tips).
- Weak attacks distract; kill lightly guarded scouts and radars before a main attack to cut their warning (TA guide).

## Commander
- Fastest builder, holds most of your resources; losing it loses a 1v1 (BAR tips, TA guide). Fight only when certain to
  survive; cloak it later in the game (TA guide). It deters early raids, but light units outrun it (BAR wiki).
